Automatic syncing

When off, the background sync (Revizto→ACC push, ACC→Revizto comment/attachment polling, auto-sync-by-filter) and incoming ACC webhooks are skipped for every project. While it's off, edits made in ACC are dropped rather than queued. Manual actions (e.g. "Link & push selected" on the Issues page) still work.
